The Linking Tool makes the connections of a network.

## How to get it

<Recipe item="linking_tool" />

## Use it

1. Right-click the first block. *First device selected* appears.
2. Right-click the second block to link them. *Devices linked.* appears.
3. To remove a link, sneak while right-clicking the second block.

Clicking the same block twice cancels the selection, and a selection expires after 10 seconds. You need access to both
blocks. Every link needs a relay at one end; see [networks and connections](/minecraft/guides/networking/).
