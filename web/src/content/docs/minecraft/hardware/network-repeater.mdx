The repeater connects things. A device can never link straight to the computer: it links to a repeater, and the
repeater links to the computer or to another repeater. Chain repeaters to reach farther.

## How to get it

<Recipe item="network_repeater" />

One recipe makes two repeaters.

## Place and connect

Place it within 32 blocks of the computer or of another relay, and link them with the
[Linking Tool](/minecraft/hardware/linking-tool/). Then link devices to it. A repeater accepts up to 16 links. Its
ports look the same on every side, so its direction does not matter.

## Configure

Its form shows its name, network status and how many of its links are connected. You can name it, though programs
rarely need to.

## Drive programs

A repeater cannot run a program.
